Deployed Platform

ProspectIQ

Which college players are worth a roster spot, a portal offer, or an NIL dollar?

Four scouting products in one deployed platform: calibrated career projections trained on 147K+ player-seasons, an Excel-fit transfer index backtested on 3,110 transfers, play-by-play scouting reports, and production-based NIL valuation.

147K+ player-seasons · 0.685 era-split ROC-AUC · 90+ endpoints Read the case study Production System

NBA Prediction Platform

Can a prediction model run itself in production, daily, with no one watching?

A daily prediction product engineered to refuse bad data: single-writer scheduling, run-pinned feature lineage, circuit-breaker guardrails, a read-only public API, and two model versions graded side by side.

7 services · unattended daily cycle · point-in-time lineage Read the case study Bayesian Modeling

International Scouting Board

What does a European stat line really mean in NBA terms?

A translation pipeline learned from the 118 players who actually made the jump, rebuilt as a joint hierarchical Bayesian model whose stricter re-evaluation displaced half of its own previous top ten.

118 transitions · 431 players ranked · 50% error cut vs naive Read the case study Counterfactual Analysis

NBA Schedule Analysis

How many wins does the schedule actually give or take?

A leakage-free counterfactual that prices every team's schedule luck with team quality held fixed, and permutation tests that retire the loudest unfair-schedule narratives.

+4.2 / -3.5 wins at the extremes · AUC 0.706 out of sample Read the case study Statistical Inference

Conference Imbalance Study

Is the East-West imbalance real, and what does it cost a team?

Sixteen seasons of standings and head-to-head records, sized with pooled rates, logistic playoff models, and a team-level permutation test designed so it could have failed.

55.7% West head-to-head · 61-point playoff-odds gap at .500 Read the case study
