What I can do, and where I demonstrated it

No skill bars and no percentages. Each group below names the projects and analyses where the capability is actually shown, because a claim without a receipt is a slogan. The complete tool inventory sits at the bottom for anyone who wants the full list.

Modeling and statistics

Bayesian hierarchical modeling with a hand-rolled, all-conjugate Gibbs sampler in the International Scouting Board. Calibrated gradient boosting with era-split evaluation in ProspectIQ, where isotonic calibration makes a 30% probability mean 30%. Permutation inference, empirical Bayes shrinkage, and reliability analysis throughout the research suite, including one analysis whose central finding is that its own target is unmeasurable noise.

Python · XGBoost · scikit-learn · statsmodels · SHAP · Gibbs sampling · empirical Bayes · Gaussian mixtures · logistic regression

Evaluation and reliability

Designs that prevent the evaluation from cheating: leakage-free pre-tipoff features in the schedule analysis, era splits with SMOTE confined to training folds in ProspectIQ, feature selection nested inside validation folds in the playoff translation analysis, and a temporally firewalled verdict set in the embeddings analysis. The research suite also went through an adversarial review pass that weakened three of my own headline claims, which are published in their corrected form.

out-of-sample and era-split designs · permutation testing · calibration · baseline benchmarking · block bootstraps · power analysis

Analytics engineering

A 97-table research database with an explicit provenance rule that no notebook may ingest its own outputs. An 860-column feature space whittled to 428 in the trajectory analysis, with every drop documented and the two worst normalization hazards caught by its own outlier screening. Every number on this site regenerates deterministically from raw data, and the research notebooks were re-executed from a clean environment before publication.

SQL · pandas · NumPy · deterministic pipelines · schema reconciliation · data quality guardrails · feature snapshots

Software and product development

Two deployed platforms. ProspectIQ: a 36-page Next.js product over an async FastAPI backend with 14 routers, serving precomputed predictions from PostgreSQL. The prediction platform: seven services under one compose file, running an unattended daily cycle with run-pinned lineage and circuit-breaker guardrails. Analysis that cannot be served is not finished.

FastAPI · PostgreSQL · Redis · Next.js · React · TypeScript · Docker Compose · rate limiting · monitoring
