    Principles

    Rules the work actually follows

    Each one is checkable against a project or analysis on this site.

    • Beat the baseline or sit out. A stat that failed its baseline was excluded from the scouting composite rather than quietly kept.
    • Evaluation should not be able to cheat. Era splits, season holdouts, fold-identical CV, and selection nested inside folds.
    • State the uncertainty. Rankings by posterior upside probability; splits that fail their permutation tests reported as noise.
    • Reproducibility is a deliverable. Seeded, deterministic pipelines regenerate every table and chart from raw data.
    • Systems should refuse to do the wrong thing. Freshness checks and circuit breakers stop the platform loudly instead of publishing quietly wrong output.
    • Write down the trade-offs. Design decisions documented with what was given up, including one shipped inconsistency kept and flagged.
    • Judge models in business units. Classification cutoffs chosen by campaign profit in dollars, not accuracy.
    • Communicate to the decision-maker. The conference imbalance project answers every question twice, technically and in plain language.
    • Leave out what does not belong. A targeting model excluded race, ethnicity, and gender on ethical and legal-risk grounds, stated in the deliverable's own recommendations.
