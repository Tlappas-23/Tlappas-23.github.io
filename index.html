    About

    The gap between promising and trustworthy

    A promising model has a good metric. A trustworthy one has survived an honest attempt to break it: out-of-sample evaluation, competition against a naive baseline, permutation tests, calibration checks. My projects are built around that distinction, and it shows in the details: a stat that failed its baseline test was excluded from a scouting composite rather than quietly kept, and a headline split that rested on 16 games was reported as noise, because it was.

    The same standard applies to systems. A pipeline that silently produces stale predictions is worse than one that stops and says why. The platforms in the case studies are built to refuse bad data: freshness enforcement, missing-data circuit breakers, point-in-time feature lineage, and monitoring that checks whether the model is still expressing uncertainty.

    I work across the full stack because analysis that cannot be served is not finished: Python and SQL for modeling, FastAPI and PostgreSQL for serving, Next.js and TypeScript for the product surface, Docker for keeping all of it reproducible.

    Matching the tool to the audience is part of the same standard. The original transfer index was a neural-network-style model built entirely in Excel, its weights fit with Solver instead of backpropagation, so a coaching staff could audit every calculation on the sheet. It now lives inside the ProspectIQ platform, serialized into the transfer API.

    Foundations

    What the master's covered

    The basketball work sits on a Villanova M.S. in Business Analytics that ran the full analytics stack, with every project graded on the model and on the business communication around it. Several assignments were solved in R and Python side by side.

    Data mining and machine learning

    A warm-lead prediction final compared logistic regression, CART, random forest, AdaBoost, and XGBoost on identical splits, then picked the winner in dollars: a threshold-optimization loop over the confusion matrix chose the cutoff that maximized campaign profit at $233,000 (XGBoost, AUC 0.916), not the one that maximized accuracy.

    Optimization

    A six-month production plan formulated as a 72-variable linear program in Excel Solver, with sensitivity analysis priced in dollars: a half-hour cut in assembly time was worth $165,000. The same Solver fluency later fit the transfer index.

    Simulation

    A discrete-event Simio model of airport check-in and security, 20+ scenarios at 50 replications each, cutting modeled passenger flow time from 68 minutes to under 18 with two added security stations and one kiosk.

    SQL and data modeling

    A purchase-pattern analysis of 155,676 grocery orders in raw SQL Server: reorder behavior, peak ordering windows, and market-basket lift, ending in shelf, inventory, and promotion recommendations.

    Forecasting and text mining

    Bike-share ridership forecast with ARIMA and with neural networks tuned over learning rates and architectures, benchmarked against a naive baseline, plus social-media text pipelines with tokenization, TF-IDF, and sentiment scoring.

    Consulting capstone

    For a national consumer research firm's survey of about 4,000 US respondents, joined Census population data, engineered the features, and clustered consumers into three actionable segments, delivered as a business report and dashboard to a real client.

    Engineering Philosophy

    Principles the projects actually follow

    Each of these is a claim you can check against the case studies or the graduate work above, not a slogan.

    Beat the baseline or sit out

    Every model competes against the naive alternative out of sample. Three-point volume failed that test and carries near-zero weight in the v2 scouting composite, and when the v3 re-evaluation applied stricter legitimacy accounting, v2's #1 target was removed from the board entirely and five of the top ten fell out of the top ten.

    Evaluation should not be able to cheat

    Leave-one-out, leave-one-season-out, fold-identical CV, and era-split designs; SMOTE confined to training folds; features restricted to what was knowable before tipoff. If the evaluation can leak, the metric is fiction.

    State the uncertainty

    The scouting board ranks by upside probability computed from 1,000 posterior draws per player. Splits ship with permutation p-values, and the ones that fail are reported as noise. Knowing when a number is quotable is part of the analysis.

    Reproducibility is a deliverable

    Deterministic scripts with fixed seeds regenerate every table and chart from raw data. Feature snapshots are pinned per run. Validation lives in notebooks anyone can re-execute, and an audit script checks the largest system end to end.

    Systems should refuse to do the wrong thing

    Freshness enforcement, missing-data circuit breakers, and a pre-tipoff blackout mean the prediction platform stops loudly instead of publishing quietly wrong output.

    Write down the trade-offs

    Design decisions are documented with what was given up: Compose-only runtime, a single production writer, duplicated feature storage. Known-limitations sections state what the models cannot see, including one inherited inconsistency kept for continuity and flagged openly.

    Judge models in business units

    The graduate data mining final chose its classification cutoff by looping over thresholds to maximize campaign profit in dollars, and optimization sensitivity was reported the same way. AUC is a diagnostic; the decision is measured in what the business actually counts.

    Communicate to the decision-maker

    Every graduate project ended in an executive summary with recommendations, and the habit carried: the conference study answers every question twice, once technically and once in plain language, because a model nobody understands funds no decision.

    Leave out what does not belong

    A school-marketing targeting model deliberately excluded race and ethnicity on ethical grounds, even though the fields were available and predictive work would have accepted them. What a model is not allowed to see is a design decision too.
