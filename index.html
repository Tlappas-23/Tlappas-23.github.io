Foundations

What the master's covered

A Villanova M.S. in Business Analytics, every project graded on the model and on the communication around it.

  • Machine learning: five classifiers on identical splits, with the cutoff chosen to maximize campaign profit rather than accuracy ($199.5K).
  • Optimization: a 72-variable production LP in Simplex, its sensitivity priced in dollars ($165K from a half-hour cut in assembly time).
  • Simulation: a discrete-event airport terminal in Simio, cutting modeled passenger flow time from 68 minutes to 17.
  • SQL and data modeling: market basket analysis of 155,676 grocery orders in raw SQL Server, ending in shelf and promotion recommendations.
  • Forecasting and text mining: ARIMA, SARIMA and recurrent-network ridership forecasts, scored against a naive baseline that ARIMA did not beat, reported as it came out.
  • Consulting capstone: a clustering segmentation of 4,000+ survey respondents joined with Census data, delivered to a real client.
